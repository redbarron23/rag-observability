# Coverage Targets

Fictional sample document for the Contoso Cloud observability programme.

## Overview

Coverage is the percentage of cloud resources that send metrics, logs and traces to the central observability platform. Targets are set per tier and per cloud.

## Tier 1

Tier 1 resources are customer-facing production workloads. The production Tier 1 coverage target is 85% within 4 months and 100% within 9 months of programme start.

## Tier 2

Tier 2 resources are internal production workloads. The Tier 2 coverage target is 70% within 6 months and 95% within 12 months.

## Current status

Azure coverage is currently 62% against a Tier 1 interim target of 85%. GCP coverage is currently 0.47% because onboarding only began last quarter; the GCP interim target is 40% by the end of next quarter and 100% for Tier 1 within 9 months.
